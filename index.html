Study in Numbers

Research Methodology

What the Results Examine

The results examine factors influencing evaluation of online reviews, whether reviews help consumers make informed purchase decisions, their importance as a source of product information, review-reading behaviour before purchases, perceived credibility and consumer trust.

Conclusion

Overall, the findings indicate variation in awareness and attitudes towards online reviews across demographic groups. The study identifies differences in how consumers evaluate, use and trust online reviews when making purchase decisions.

The findings should also be interpreted in light of the study's stated limitations, including sample size and geographic coverage.
