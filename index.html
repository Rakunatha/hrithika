01 / 10 A Study on the Impact of Online Reviews on Consumer Purchase Decisions An empirical study examining how consumers evaluate, use and trust online reviews while making purchase decisions. Research Presentation 02 / 10 Study in Numbers The research reports an empirical study based on 224 respondents from diverse demographic backgrounds. The study examines awareness and attitudes towards online reviews across demographic and behavioural variables. 224 Respondents 03 / 10 Research Methodology The study uses a structured questionnaire and empirical analysis. The questionnaire examines demographic characteristics together with respondents' perceptions and behaviour concerning online reviews. Primary Empirical Study 04 / 10 What the Results Examine The results examine factors influencing evaluation of online reviews, whether reviews help consumers make informed purchase decisions, their importance as a source of product information, review-reading behaviour before purchases, perceived credibility and consumer trust. The study identifies differences in how consumers evaluate, use and trust online reviews when making purchase decisions.
