02 / 12 — At a Glance

Study at a Glance

The study examines the impact of online reviews on consumer purchase decisions using empirical and descriptive research. A total of 224 respondents were studied through structured questionnaires using a five-point Likert scale.

5

Empirical

SPSS

03 / 12 — Objectives

Objectives of the Study

  • 01 To study the impact of online reviews on consumer purchase decisions.
  • 02 To understand consumer awareness and attitudes towards online reviews.
  • 03 To examine the perceived credibility of online reviews.
  • 04 To understand how different demographic and behavioural factors influence consumers' responses to online reviews.
04 / 12 — Literature Review

Literature Foundations

Chevalier & Mayzlin (2006)
Positive online reviews significantly influence product sales, while negative reviews can reduce purchase intentions.
Ghose & Ipeirotis (2010)
Review quality, readability and linguistic characteristics influence consumer trust.
Filieri (2016)
Review content, writing style, extremity and website reputation affect trustworthiness.
Mayzlin, Dover & Chevalier (2014)
Fake and promotional reviews can reduce consumer trust and affect purchasing behaviour.

The literature consistently highlights the importance of review quality, credibility, usefulness and authenticity in consumer decision-making.

05 / 12 — Methodology

Empirical & Descriptive Design

The study follows empirical and descriptive research. Data were collected using structured questionnaires administered through convenience sampling. The questionnaire incorporated a five-point Likert scale to measure attitudes and perceptions.

224
Respondents collected through convenience sampling
5
Independent variables: age, gender, education, geographic area and occupation
1
Dependent variable: awareness and attitude towards online reviews

11 / 12 — Discussion

Discussion of Findings

  • 01 The findings indicate that consumers actively consider online reviews when making purchasing decisions.
  • 02 Review credibility is an important consideration in how consumers evaluate online information.
  • 03 Different demographic and behavioural groups display different patterns in their responses to online reviews.
  • 04 The findings are consistent with prior research emphasising the importance of authentic, informative and useful reviews.
12 / 12 — Conclusion

Conclusion & Recommendations

Online reviews form an important source of information for consumers and can influence purchase-related attitudes and decisions.

Recommendation: Businesses and online platforms should encourage authentic, informative and trustworthy customer feedback.

The study is based on convenience sampling and 224 respondents, so future research may benefit from larger and more diverse samples.
